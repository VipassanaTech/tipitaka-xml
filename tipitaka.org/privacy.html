Privacy Policy

Pali Tipitaka – read‑only app from Tipitaka.org
Effective date: May 19, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile application “Pali Tipitaka” (referred to as “the App”), which displays the complete Pali Canon from the 6th Buddhist Council held in Myanmar. The App is a read‑only viewer for the textual content provided by Tipitaka.org (the official website of the Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana Tipitaka).

1. No User Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information from its users. Specifically:

  • No user accounts, login, or registration.
  • No forms, comments, search logs, or any mechanism to input user data.
  • No tracking of location, device identifiers (e.g. Advertising ID, IMEI), or browsing behaviour inside the App.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or performance monitoring tools are integrated.

The App merely requests internet access to fetch and display the public Pali Canon texts, which are hosted on tipitaka.org in 14 languages/scripts. No data is sent back to the App developer or any third party.

2. Internet Permission & Data Flow

The only permission used by the App is INTERNET. This allows the App to download the textual content from Tipitaka.org for display. All communications are read‑only and initiated solely by the App. Tipitaka.org’s own server logs may collect standard web request data (e.g. IP address, timestamp) as part of normal website operation – that data is not accessible to, nor controlled by, the App developer.

3. Third‑Party Services

None. The App does not integrate any third‑party libraries or SDKs that collect data. This includes:

  • No advertising networks (AdMob, Meta Audience Network, etc.)
  • No analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • No crash reporters (Crashlytics, Sentry, etc.)
  • No social media or login SDKs (Facebook, Google Sign-In, etc.)

If future versions of the App add any such services, this privacy policy will be updated immediately, and a new version will be published on Google Play.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because the App collects no personal data of any kind, it is safe for children of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone under 13 years of age. No special parental consent is required, as no data processing occurs.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page, with the “Effective date” revised accordingly. We encourage users to review this page periodically. Continued use of the App after modifications constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
